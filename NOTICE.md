Age of War was created by **Louissi** and published by **Max Games**. Original artwork, sounds and the
level/unit design are theirs. The soundtrack *Glorious Morning* is by **Waterflame**.

The repository owner states that they asked the rights holders (including Waterflame for the music)
and received their agreement to use the original assets in this fan port. That permission is the
owner's to document; it is not verified by this repository. If you are a rights holder and want
anything changed or removed, open an issue.

The code in this repository (engine, simulation port, networking, UI shell, tooling) is MIT-licensed
where it is original work; the extracted game assets in `public/orig/` remain the property of their owners.
